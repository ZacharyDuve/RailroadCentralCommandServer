Routes generally can be though up of as a graph. 

## How can we think of the graph for road vehicles

### Roads

Roads is something that takes you between two points. Either of the two points could be a dead end or an intersection

Roads also could consist of one or more lanes.

Roads have a length and speed associated to them

### Intersection

A place where two different roads meet. Has possible paths through the intersection. With one active one

Intersections and paths.

Challenges with trains is that there are limited paths a train can go.
Also only one path is valid at a time
